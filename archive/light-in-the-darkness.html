Light in the Darkness

By: Andina Azya Rahmadani
About the Work

Light in the Darkness portrays how someone can become a source of strength and hope during difficult moments. Through the image of light in the darkness, the poem expresses how inspiration from someone admired can provide comfort, courage, and a reason to keep moving forward.

A Note

Light in the Darkness was written from a quiet feeling of gratitude toward someone whose presence became meaningful in my everyday life. Sometimes, even from a distance, someone's smile, words, or presence can make difficult days feel a little lighter.
