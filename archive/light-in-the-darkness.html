02 · About the Work

Light in the Darkness is a personal poem about finding strength, comfort, and hope through the presence of someone who unknowingly became a source of motivation.

03 · Inspiration

The poem was inspired by an idol who motivated me to get through everyday life. There were moments when I felt like I had no one around me. Seeing him smile through social media reminded me that I still had someone to look forward to, even though he does not know that I exist.
