My Lost Dreams

By: Andina Azya Rahmadani
About the Work

My Lost Dreams reflects on the feeling of losing a dream that once felt certain. Through uncertainty and self-doubt, the poem follows the search for meaning after that dream has disappeared, and the hope of finding a reason to keep searching.

A Note

My Lost Dreams was born from a dream I once carried quietly, but never truly had the chance to pursue. For a long time, the voices around me made it feel distant. Then, through the words of someone I admired, I was reminded that some dreams are worth chasing, even after we have almost let them go. This poem holds the moment I chose to look toward that dream once again, quietly hoping it might still find its way to me.
